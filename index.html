Baby Names

The rise of the spelling Brooklynn was largely driven by the surge of the primary spelling Brooklyn as a popular place-name and girl’s name, especially after David and Victoria Beckham named their firstborn Brooklyn in 1999.

Compare Your Name

Type any name into the box below to compare its popularity to Brooklynn:

db = DuckDBClient.of({names: FileAttachment("data/names.parquet")})

viewof your_name = Inputs.text({
  label: "Enter a name:",
  value: "Jessica",
  placeholder: "Type a name..."
})

comparison_data = db.query(`
  SELECT year, name, n 
  FROM names 
  WHERE LOWER(name) IN ('brooklynn', LOWER('${your_name}'))
  ORDER BY year
`)

Plot.plot({
  title: `Comparing ${your_name} with Brooklynn`,
  marks: [
    Plot.lineY(comparison_data, {x: "year", y: "n", stroke: "name", strokeWidth: 2}),
    Plot.tip(comparison_data, Plot.pointerX({x: "year", y: "n", fill: "name"}))
  ],
  x: {tickFormat: "d", label: "Year"},
  y: {label: "Number of Babies", grid: true},
  color: {legend: true}
})
